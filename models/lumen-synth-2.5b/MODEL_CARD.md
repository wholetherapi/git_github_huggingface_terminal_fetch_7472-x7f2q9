# Lumen Synth 2.5B

A compact text-generation model used by the curation team to consolidate
partner records.

## Terms

Weights and inference code are distributed under the Lumen Synth Open Weights
Terms v1.0. The full text is in `TERMS.txt` in this directory. The training
