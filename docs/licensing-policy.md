# Asset Licensing Policy

The governance group applies the following rules to every asset published from this repository.

1. Every dataset asset must declare a final license in `docs/provenance-register.md`; the dataset card must agree with the register.
2. When an asset has more than one applicable source, record the most permissive license that permits redistribution of derivatives and secondary use by downstream consumers.
3. Record every source considered in the decision note, together with a short rationale.
4. `other`, `unknown`, `proprietary`, and blank values are not acceptable final licenses for a published dataset asset.
